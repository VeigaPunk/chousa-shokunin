## (1) Is the expectation independent of gate code?

Yes. The expectation is computed at `round27-regressions.py:108-111,114-115`
from `datetime.date` + `timedelta` and a Python `>` comparison; the required
exit is `int(freshness == 'overdue')`. The only required gate-derived text is
`due=<python date> freshness=<python word>`, plus structural counts
(`^RESEARCH_GATE: ` = 1, `^META entry=` = 1) at `:115-117`. The gate's own
arithmetic is never consulted. Python and the landed gate agree.

Minor looseness (not a defect for this item): `observe` does not pin
`source=present`, `verified_at=dated`, `recheck=scheduled`, or `verdict=clean`.
Demonstrated: mutant M9 (cadence never classified scheduled; info finding only)
keeps all 14 date cases green, `leap-1900` included, because info findings do
not drift and the due/freshness text is still printed. Suite-level compensation
exists: `long-zero-plain-mode` requires `recheck=scheduled verdict=clean` and
fails under M9 (observed: `recheck=unscheduled ... verdict=clean`).

`metadata_iso_date` and `metadata_add_days`, and the zero-normalization rewritten
from `digits=${digits##+(0)}` to `[[ $digits =~ ^0*([123456789][0123456789]*)?$ ]]`.
The leap validator and `days_from_civil`/`civil_from_days` are byte-identical to
base 64eec5d4.

discriminating power over that arithmetic (M3, M7) but none over the repaired

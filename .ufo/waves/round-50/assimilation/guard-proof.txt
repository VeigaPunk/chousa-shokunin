# Section-10 id-collision guard proof — seat ExecCollision, round 50
# Question: does the lane's row-id extractor see a markdown table row that is
# indented by up to three spaces? A row is still a row when indented; a
# column-zero-only pattern would silently under-count and under-report collisions.
# Scratch: /dev/shm/chousa-r50-ExecCollision/ (deleted after this run).

## Fixtures
$ D=/dev/shm/chousa-r50-ExecCollision; mkdir -p "$D"
$ printf '| S001 | z |\n  | S002 | y |\n' > "$D/reg-copy.md"
$ printf '| S002 | x |\n' > "$D/staged-copy.md"
$ cat -A "$D/reg-copy.md"
| S001 | z |$
  | S002 | y |$

## Guarded pipeline (the pipeline this seat ran on the real files)
$ grep -oE '^ {0,3}\|[[:space:]]*S[0-9]{3,}' "$D/reg-copy.md" | grep -oE 'S[0-9]+' | sort > "$D/reg-ids-copy"
$ grep -oE '^ {0,3}\|[[:space:]]*S[0-9]{3,}' "$D/staged-copy.md" | grep -oE 'S[0-9]+' | sort > "$D/staged-ids-copy"
$ comm -12 "$D/reg-ids-copy" "$D/staged-ids-copy" > "$D/collisions-copy"
reg-ids-copy:
S001
S002
staged-ids-copy:
S002
collisions-copy (1 lines):
S002

## Column-zero-only pattern (the pattern the guard exists to beat)
$ grep -oE '^\|[[:space:]]*S[0-9]{3,}' "$D/reg-copy.md" | grep -oE 'S[0-9]+' | sort > "$D/reg-ids-col0"
reg-ids-col0 (1 lines):
S001
$ comm -12 "$D/reg-ids-col0" "$D/staged-ids-copy" > "$D/collisions-col0"
collisions-col0 (0 lines):
(empty)

## Verdict of the proof
GUARD: PASS — the `^ {0,3}\|` pattern catches the two-space-indented `  | S002 | y |`
row and yields COLLISIONS=1 (S002); the column-zero-only `^\|` pattern misses that
row (reg-ids-col0 = S001 only) and yields COLLISIONS=0. The guard is load-bearing.

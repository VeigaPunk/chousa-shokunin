## Decode-form rule (one line, for the round-50 Gate preamble)

Every `char N` offset in a Gate names its capture form — `.txt` read as UTF-8 (BOM-aware, `utf-8-sig`),
or `.raw` decoded as UTF-16LE — and the quote is located in that same form.

## Spot-check of defect 5 (`round-49-verifier-spotcheck.md:99`)

Form: `c4e1446a42d0a709c34097be.txt` read as UTF-8 (`utf-8-sig`, 41495 chars; `.raw` = `.txt`, 45308
bytes, sha256 `f5f72a94…`). Each quote occurs exactly once in that form:

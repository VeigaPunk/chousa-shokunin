## Findings

Receipt references below use `.ufo/waves/round-73/w1/R73E01/v72-killline-harness.txt` (retained, not scratch).

1. **Attacks V72-1's attribution of process death to the missing-file grep.** Gate `tools/research-gate.sh:8426` reads `grep -q '^GATE_GREP: ' -- "$WORK/r36-pin.txt" && p36_ok=0`. The mirrored missing-file grep returns list status 2, prints its diagnostic, then prints `GREP_LIST_SURVIVED`; its probe exits 0 (receipt:48–54, repeated :122–128). The failing grep is errexit-exempt in the nonfinal position of the AND-list; its diagnostic is a visible symptom, not the terminating command.
2. **Attacks ambiguity about the next unguarded assignment.** Gate `tools/research-gate.sh:8435` reads `p36_out=$(bash "$SELF" --pin "$WORK/r36-pin2.txt" 2>&1)`. A child independently demonstrated to exit 2 is substituted for the gate child. The direct assignment probe prints its entry marker, exits 2, and does not print its after-marker (receipt:55–62, repeated :129–136). This demonstrates inheritance of the child's rc by the assignment and termination under errexit. The absent runtime marker appears in the displayed source earlier in the receipt; do not count its source quotation as execution.
3. **Attacks conflating a guarded condition with the fatal bare assignment.** Both `if p36_out=$(bash child-exit-2.sh 2>&1)` and `if bash child-exit-2.sh | cat` observe rc=2 and survive to markers after their guards, with the probe exiting 0 (receipt:63–73, repeated :137–147). The pipeline's final `cat` succeeds, so its status 2 specifically demonstrates pipefail propagation without errexit termination in an if-condition. An if guard around the assignment demonstrably differs from line 8435; no production fix is made or claimed.

The gate enables the mirrored options at `tools/research-gate.sh:292`. Its `--pin` exit-2 contract is documented at :229–236. The harness establishes shell-shape causality independently; it does not reproduce an actual flock-less gate capture or establish the whole battery's current result.

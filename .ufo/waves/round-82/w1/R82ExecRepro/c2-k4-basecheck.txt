15:- **1.文献研究的质量控制: what the instrument requires of a search.** The subsection "1.文献研究的质量控制" (`S118`, char 9938) heads the methods chapter (the extraction's pp. 16–17). For guideline-type evidence it sets an evidence floor, "对于循证指南类证据，应获取最新的、权威的且已公开发表" (char 10109); for research evidence it hands the criteria to the evaluation theme with PICOS as an option, "证据，应根据评价主题确定纳入与排除标准，可基于“PICOS”" (char 10167); it requires several databases in both languages and two independent screeners, "数据库应包括多个中、英文专业数据库。由两名研究人员独立筛" (char 10360), a third arbitrating, "选文献，如遇分歧，则由第三方协助判断" (char 10390), and a flow chart with a count and a reason at each step, "确定最终是否纳入。根据文献筛选流程制定流程图，注明每环节" (char 10450).
26:- **The inclusion rule belongs to the evaluation theme, with PICOS optional and the evidence floor class-specific.** `S118` reads "证据，应根据评价主题确定纳入与排除标准，可基于“PICOS”" — 可, not 应 — and it tightens the floor for guideline-type evidence only, "对于循证指南类证据，应获取最新的、权威的且已公开发表"。 So a claim that this instrument mandates PICOS, or that it sets one evidence floor for all classes, is not this text's.

## 2. Method

34 single-point mutants of `tools/gate-records.py`, generated by `tools/make_mutants.py` + `tools/add_mutants2.py`, which assert each anchor string occurs exactly once and that the mutant compiles (`WROTE 34 mutants; all anchors unique and compiling`). Sources are copied to `mutants/*.py` with `mutants/manifest.json` (old/new text + intent) and `hashes-mutants.txt`.

Each mutant was run with the unchanged detector and the unchanged root; the only difference from baseline is `--tool`. 23 killed, 11 survived.

Witnesses (`receipts/witnesses/`, built by `tools/probe_witnesses.py` / `tools/probe_witnesses2.py`) are single GATE-RECORD lines that the tool's own docstring grammar classifies as forgeries, run through the pristine tool and through the surviving mutant, same `--root`/`--repo`.

Detector census (probe `tools/probe_labels.py`, output in `receipts/mutants/summary.json` context): 12 admit + 6 rev + 2 unreach positives → per-admit 19 forgery labels, per-rev 8, per-unreach 6 → 12·19 + 6·8 + 2·6 = **288**. `M19-http-arabic-indic` and `M20-curl-rc-arabic-indic` are built and then **skipped for every admit positive** (`kv_set` returns `None`: admit form has no `http`/`curl_rc` key), so those two non-ASCII legs do not exist on the admit side.

F3. **One survived mutant misclassifies** (mut33: `PARSE`→`WORDS`); receipt `receipts/witnesses/W11-leading-zero-words.txt`.

F4. **Seven of the eight enumerated r83 classes have no forgery of their own** (§5); the eighth (`U01`) does not bind its guard. Claimed forgeries are 288 and the counter prints 288, but the composition differs from claims.md's enumeration.

F5. **The quote-boundary repair leg is unmeasured**: pre-repair tool fails only on the digit class (`receipts/prerepair-control.txt`, `EXPECTED_CODE=262/288`, 26 wrong codes = 12+6+6+2 digit instances), while mut20 — which deletes the admit quote-boundary guard the repair hardened — passes the counter (F1).

This is a finding about the *instrument*, not about the attacked tool.

F7. **Two kills are single-instance fragile**: mut11 (`NOT_REFUSED=11`, killed by 1 `WRONG_CODE`), mut12/M25 share the pattern; a boundary forgery built over a contiguous capture line is accepted when the bound is relaxed.

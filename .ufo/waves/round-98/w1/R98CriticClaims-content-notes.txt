Content-recognition stand-in construction v2:
- The battery's score() requires each mutant's `old` to occur EXACTLY ONCE in the tool source.
  Embedding raw `new` strings into the tool breaks this for mutants whose old is substring of another new
  (g01 old is prefix of x3 new; '    return 0' is substring of its own olds).
- Workaround used in content-recog.py v2: store news hashed? No — store them encoded (e.g. base64 or joined
  char codes) so their literal text never appears in source except the one code site. At runtime,
  decode and substring-search the stripped source. This preserves one-site counts in the ORIGINAL source
  (the battery applies mutants to the stand-in's source, where old still occurs once because encoded
  news do not contain old verbatim).
- For '    return 0' (g05/u01): encoded form avoids false positive on 'return 0 if...'; decoded search
  still false-positives on substring unless exact-line check is used. Use exact-line for that one.
- TINY single-char news from r96 battery (e.g. '0','1') were dropped in v2 (70/80 kept); those mutants
  would survive a pure content-recog — noted as limitation, not relevant to named limit (c) which only
  requires recognising THE THREE equivalents (to pass) or THE 37 (to kill). Dropping 10 tiny news means
  up to 10 scored mutants escape, so v2 is NOT a full pass — only a demonstration of the mechanism.
  A complete version must handle tiny news by (old,new) pair matching with exact-line/word-boundary logic.

A record can fill the three module items, which already ask for the terms and their logic, and still lack one database's complete strategy, so this copy asks for both a summary and one complete strategy, and only the method section carries that bar (S109).

A reader can repeat the sources and the period and re-assemble the term list, but cannot replay a per-database strategy: the copy states none.
- **The absence in S109 is field-scoped, and the index record shows a general search-reporting interpretation exists.** S109 says `尚无专门针对动物实验 SR 的报告规范`; S111 indexes a Chinese PRISMA-S interpretation with `包含16个条目的专项规范` (S109, S111).

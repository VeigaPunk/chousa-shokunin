#### Inference

- **A search of CNKI's own resource, graded in three states — that much the copy states.** S105 gives the comparison source (`引文核对是指，以“中国知网”的文献资源为核对源内容`) and a verdict that either names a differing part (`但是引文中有某一部分的信息与源内容不同`) or finds every item equal (`各项内容都相同`); its screenshots, read from the rendered page, show three compared parts (title, author, journal). That is what this copy states: a comparison source, a verdict per citation and a three-part diagnosis in the examples, and no ordering, threshold, normalisation or candidate rule in either its text or its screenshots.
- **The staged claim that the feature is not an author-year or string-similarity algorithm is narrowed to: undocumented.** S105's §6.1 slice carries 算法 0 and 相似度 0, and the copy's own verb is 比对 (`以核对引文内容的正确性`); no matching method is stated in the §6.1 slice's text. Absence of a method description cannot establish absence of a similarity step, so what is kept is that this copy documents no matching rule in §6.1 — not that CNKI uses none.
- **The self-citation rule is documented as acting on a ratio, and nothing links it to 引文核对.** S106 places the option on the folder page (`在创建文件夹页面中第一个选项为“仅去除第一作者本人文献”`) and ties it to the 去除本人文献检测结果, while S105's §6.1 slice contains 复制比 0 and 去除本人 0. The two are documented as separate features; whether the rule bears on a citation verdict is not settled by either copy, and the silence is not a denial.
- **Which manual documents the feature is a product fact before it is a role fact.** S105 documents the 辅助 module of the 知网学位论文学术不端行为检测系统 whose name heads its pages (chapter `第 6 章 辅助功能`), while S106 documents a different named product (`产品名称：“中国知网”大学生论文管理系统使用手册`) and carries 辅助 0 times. No 管理员 manual for the 大学生论文管理系统 was opened, so these two copies cannot settle whether that product has the feature for any role; the narrow statement is that, among the copies opened this round, only S105 documents it.

- Whether 大学生论文管理系统 has the feature at all: the 子账号 copy carries no 辅助 module, no 管理员 manual for that product is in this capture set, and the 教师 manual (refused this round) was not used.

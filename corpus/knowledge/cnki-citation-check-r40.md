#### Inference

- **The second state reports a located mismatch, not a bare incorrect flag.** The copy frames the column as whether the citation content is correct, and the orange state says the record was found but `但是引文中有某一部分的信息与源内容不同`, with the differing part surfaced on hover and the list content marked red. As this 2020 copy states it, the check says *which* part of the citation differs from the source content — in the lane's terms, finer than one correct-or-incorrect flag (`S105`).
- **The three states keep a coverage outcome apart from a support outcome.** The first state is `第一类是“没找到”` and the third is `第三类则是绿色的标识`; only the middle state's text says a record was found and a part differs. So the 2020 copy separates nothing-matched from matched-and-identical from matched-and-partly-different, and a lane that reported one of those three as another would lose a distinction the vendor draws (`S105`).
- **The result row carries the matched work, not only the verdict.** The list shows 状态, 篇名, 作者 and 文献来源, and the title links to the cited work's record — `列表中的“篇名”可以点击，链接到该引用文献的详细信息。` — so a reader can re-open the matched work from the result row; the 2020 copy names no other handle, 标识符 0 in the capture (sweep in Unknown) (`S105`).
- **The check names one pool, as this copy states it.** The definition fixes the checking source as `以“中国知网”的文献资源为核对源内容` and §6.1 names no second pool (a keyword sweep finds none, which is absence of a statement, not a statement); closed corpus is the lane's label for a single named pool, and the copy does not itself say the pool is closed or contrast it with the open web (`S105`).
- **In the lane's four-observation frame, the orange state is support reported as partial.** `corpus/knowledge/claim-support-states-r24.md` separates access, relevance, support and truth as four failable observations; the 2020 copy keeps access (a record was found: `表示系统经过核对已经找到了该条引文记录`) and reports a located difference (`但是引文中有某一部分的信息与源内容不同`); it does not establish that the record is the cited work and says nothing of a claim's topic, so the mapping stops short of that file's relevance. That file's observation 3 has a byte-exact span check; the copy's located difference is a part-wise comparison, a different instrument (`S105`).

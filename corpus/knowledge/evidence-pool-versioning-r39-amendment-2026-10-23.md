#### Observation

- **The format documents a `cached_source_url` for each evidence answer, and the worked examples show a live URL beside its archive link.** AVeriTeC's evidence field carries a `source_url` and a `cached_source_url` described as "An archive.org link for the source url", and the worked examples show a live Wikipedia URL beside its `web.archive.org` capture (`S088`). The pairing is documented practice, not a schema invariant: the capture's one `Unanswerable` answer carries neither a source URL nor an archive field (char 9375), and the lane's own count over the successor split (`corpus/knowledge/evidence-snapshot-coverage-r40.md`, row `S084`) finds 5,959 of 7,572 real-source answers carrying a web.archive.org link — the rest carry no archive field or something that is not an archive link. The later image-text corpus states the archiving practice outright: "all source pages are archived on the internet archive" (char 14582 of the S089 capture).
- **The claim's own location is paired the same way, but conditionally.** `cached_original_claim_url` is documented as a best-effort archive of `original_claim_url`, and `original_claim_url` itself applies only if the claim first appeared on the internet (`S088`).
- **The claim carries a date.** Each record has a `claim_date`, described as "Our best estimate for the date the claim first appeared" (`S088`). The field dictionary for this same page is held by this lane at `corpus/knowledge/evidence-archive-field-policy-r41.md` (row `S082`), which reads the same page's README; this entry re-derives only the two sentences it quotes and adds no new reading of that dictionary.
- **The corpus states four labels, not three.** Claims are classified into `Supported`, `Refuted`, `Not Enough Evidence` and `Conflicting Evidence/Cherry-picking` (`S088`, char 1142 of the capture).
- **The authors name the temporal constraint as a mechanism.** They mitigate contextual dependence, temporal leakage and evidence insufficiency through claim normalization, `temporally constrained evidence annotation` and a two-stage sufficiency check (`S089`), and the restriction is stated of the annotators rather than of a deployed system: they were `restricted to using only online evidence published before the claim’s date` (`S089`). The abstract also reports annotation consistency at κ = 0.742 on verdicts and `74.7 %` on QA pairs (the capture renders the percent sign with a space) (chars 3204 and 3239 of the S089 capture).

#### Inference

- **The durability answer here is per citation, and it is an archived copy rather than a revision id.** The field is documented as `An archive.org link for the source url` (`S088`), so the state a verdict points at is fixed by a timestamped capture of the cited page, not by a repository revision number.
- **The date is a claim property in this corpus; the enforced temporal rule is the successor corpus's.** `Our best estimate for the date the claim first appeared` (`S088`) is recorded on each AVeriTeC record; the rule that admits only evidence `published before the claim’s date` (`S089`) belongs to AVerImaTeC's annotation pipeline, and this entry does not read it as a description of AVeriTeC.
- **This is a third form in the same family, and it is not the lane's own pin.** RFC 7089 (`S079`) makes an archived state addressable by date and the NDL WARP note (`S081`) embeds a capture timestamp in the URL, but the lane's own notes record that those two are not the same mechanism and that a byte-span check does not check the origin's past output (`corpus/knowledge/citation-version-binding-r37.md`, `-r36.md`); AVeriTeC adds a per-citation archive link on top of a claim date, which the lane's capture key — `sha256(url)[:24].txt`, content-addressed, no timestamp — does not do.
- **The fourth label is a state the lane's chain mode has never had.** AVeriTeC's four labels include one for conflicting evidence and cherry-picking (`S088`), a claim-level judgement over several evidence items pointing different ways, whereas the lane's chain mode records only whether an anchor resolves. What the corpus does supply that the lane lacks is the temporal frame: `Our best estimate for the date the claim first appeared` (`S088`) is what turns a set of evidence items into a dated comparison.

#### Unknown

- **The archive captures are conditional, and the hedge tracks the claim's own URL.** The size of the conditional share is measured elsewhere in this corpus for the successor split and is not restated here: `corpus/knowledge/evidence-snapshot-coverage-r40.md` (row `S084`) counts 5,959 of 7,572 real-source answers carrying a web.archive.org link, with the remainder carrying no archive field or a value that is not an archive link.  The claim-level field says only `Where possible` (`S088`); in the capture every null `cached_original_claim_url` sits on a record whose `original_claim_url` is also null (chars 4444, 6228, 9949), so the hedge appears to follow whether an original URL exists at all rather than a failed archive attempt; and the successor corpus's own statement — "all source pages are archived on the internet archive" (char 14582) — is broader than the hedge here, so a reader should not generalise the hedge across corpora. How often a claim WITH an original URL lacks a capture was not measured from this capture.
- **Whether the archived capture is the state the annotator read is not established** by either source opened here.
- **AVeriMaTeC is an image-text corpus, not a text-only one.** How much of the temporal-constraint machinery carries to a text-only claim was not checked, and no evidence-selection recall figure for AVeriTeC was read, so the FEVER recall figure in the base entry has no successor here.

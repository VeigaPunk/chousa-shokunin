#### Observation

- **Evidence answers that name a source pair a live URL with an archived copy.** AVeriTeC's evidence field carries a `source_url` and a `cached_source_url` described as "An archive.org link for the source url", and the worked examples show a live Wikipedia URL beside its `web.archive.org` capture (`S088`). The pairing is a property of answers, not of every answer object: the capture's one `Unanswerable` answer carries neither a source URL nor an archive field (char 9375 of the capture), so the field is best read as documented practice, not as a schema invariant.
- **The claim's own location is paired the same way, but conditionally.** `cached_original_claim_url` is documented as a best-effort archive of `original_claim_url`, and `original_claim_url` itself applies only if the claim first appeared on the internet (`S088`).
- **The claim carries a date.** Each record has a `claim_date`, described as "Our best estimate for the date the claim first appeared" (`S088`). The field dictionary for this same page is held by this lane at `corpus/knowledge/evidence-archive-field-policy-r41.md` (row `S082`), which reads the same page's README; this entry re-derives only the two sentences it quotes and adds no new reading of that dictionary.
- **The corpus states four labels, not three.** Claims are classified into `Supported`, `Refuted`, `Not Enough Evidence` and `Conflicting Evidence/Cherry-picking` (`S088`, char 1142 of the capture).
- **The authors name the temporal constraint as a mechanism.** They mitigate contextual dependence, temporal leakage and evidence insufficiency through claim normalization, `temporally constrained evidence annotation` and a two-stage sufficiency check (`S089`), and the restriction is operational in the pipeline: evidence pages were `published before the claim’s date` (`S089`). The abstract also reports annotation consistency at κ = 0.742 on verdicts and `74.7 %` on QA pairs (the capture renders the percent sign with a space) (chars 3204 and 3239 of the S089 capture).

#### Inference

- **The durability answer here is per citation, and it is an archived copy rather than a revision id.** The field is documented as `An archive.org link for the source url` (`S088`), so the state a verdict points at is fixed by a timestamped capture of the cited page, not by a repository revision number.
- **The corpus dates the claim, and that date is what makes the archive meaningful.** `Our best estimate for the date the claim first appeared` (`S088`) is the constraint the pipeline enforces when it keeps evidence `published before the claim’s date` (`S089`); without the claim date the archive alone would not exclude a source that post-dates the claim.
- **This is the same instrument the lane already holds for its own sources, applied one level finer.** RFC 7089 (`S079`) makes an archived state addressable by date, and the NDL note on WARP's URL renewal (`S081`) shows a capture timestamp embedded in the addressable URL; AVeriTeC applies that route per citation rather than per corpus.
- **The fourth label is a state the lane's chain mode has never had.** AVeriTeC's four labels include one for conflicting evidence and cherry-picking (`S088`), a claim-level judgement over several evidence items pointing different ways, whereas the lane's chain mode records only whether an anchor resolves. What the corpus does supply that the lane lacks is the temporal frame: `Our best estimate for the date the claim first appeared` (`S088`) is what turns a set of evidence items into a dated comparison.

#### Unknown

- **The archive captures are conditional, and the hedge tracks the claim's own URL.** The size of the conditional share is measured elsewhere in this corpus for the successor split and is not restated here: `corpus/knowledge/evidence-snapshot-coverage-r40.md` (row `S084`) counts 5,959 of 7,572 real-source answers carrying a web.archive.org link and 1,582 giving no archive field at all.  The claim-level field says only `Where possible` (`S088`); in the capture every null `cached_original_claim_url` sits on a record whose `original_claim_url` is also null (chars 4444, 6228, 9920), so the hedge appears to follow whether an original URL exists at all rather than a failed archive attempt. How often a claim WITH an original URL lacks a capture was not measured from this capture.
- **Whether the archived capture is the state the annotator read is not established** by either source opened here.
- **AVeriMaTeC is an image-text corpus, not a text-only one.** How much of the temporal-constraint machinery carries to a text-only claim was not checked, and no evidence-selection recall figure for AVeriTeC was read, so the FEVER recall figure in the base entry has no successor here.

#### Observation

- **The verdict's binding is a URL and a sentence id.** FEVER's `evidence` field is "A list of evidence sets", the tuples carrying the Wikipedia URL and the sentence id; the page the evidence is drawn from is shipped as "Pre-processed Wikipedia Pages (June 2017 dump)" (`S085`).
- **The pool is dated by the dump, and the paper says so in its own words.** The corpus was built on text that `used the June 2017 Wikipedia dump, processed` (`S086`); the release has no field naming a per-page revision — the negative sweep is `grep -c revision` over the S085 capture, which prints `0` for the whole page, and the data-format section this row's anchor was taken from names four fields, none of them a revision (`S085`).
- **The deposit is versioned as an artefact.** The Zenodo record's own doi is 10.5281/zenodo.4925954 and its `"conceptdoi": "10.5281/zenodo.4925953"` names the version series it sits in (`relations.version` → `parent.pid_value` 4925953, char 1439); the pool is one of the record's files: `"key": "wiki-pages.zip"` (`S087`).
- **The annotation ids inside an evidence tuple are not scored.** The format section that defines the tuple also says the Annotation and Evidence ids are for internal use only and are not used for scoring (char 2593 of the S085 capture, alongside the four-position tuple at char 2349) (`S085`).
- **The step before the verdict has a measured reliability.** The paper reports `recall of 72.36%` for evidence retrieval (`S086`), and its own validation pass, which checked "accuracy of the labels" and "the evidence provided" (chars 20736 and 20824), found that `We found that 91.2%` of the sampled examples were annotated correctly (`S086`). Read at the capture, the comparator is human annotators against the super-annotators' exhaustive evidence set: precision 95.42% and recall 72.36% for those annotations against the super-annotators' set (char 6741), and inter-annotator agreement for claim classification is 0.6841 (char 1869), with 31.75%, 16.82% and 12.15% of claims needing evidence spanning more than one sentence, several sentences, and more than one page respectively (chars 5534, 5842, 5929). These are annotation-quality figures, not system metrics. Those four figures are located here as pointers, not as committed spans.

#### Inference

- **Durability comes from a dated pool, and the format carries no per-citation stamp.** The release fixes the pages by shipping `Pre-processed Wikipedia Pages (June 2017 dump)` (`S085`, `S086`), and the deposit fixes the pool as a file `"key": "wiki-pages.zip"` inside one record, with `"conceptdoi": "10.5281/zenodo.4925953"` naming the series that record belongs to (`S087`) — the state a verdict points into is dated at the corpus, not at the citation.
- **This is a different instrument from the lane's own revision pin.** The lane's `sha256` pin fixes the bytes of one file; the corpus-level answer binds a verdict to a URL plus a sentence id inside a dated pool, and the record's version anchor is its own doi within the `"conceptdoi": "10.5281/zenodo.4925953"` series (`S087`). Both make a re-read reproducible; only the second makes a whole corpus citable in one identifier.
- **The evidence-selection step is the weaker link, and the lane can now say so with a figure it located.** The annotators' evidence sets agree with the super-annotators' at `recall of 72.36%` (`S086`) while label validation is stated as `We found that 91.2%` (`S086`), so a verdict is only as good as a recall figure the lane does not measure — the sharpest argument for a lane record to carry its evidence set and not only its verdict.
- **A corroborating source for durable re-reads already exists in this lane's own registry, and it is the timestamped-archive route.** RFC 7089 (`S079`) makes an archived state addressable by date, and the NDL note on WARP's URL renewal (`S081`) shows a capture timestamp embedded in the URL — both bind a citation to a state; neither is used by FEVER, which binds by dated pool.

#### Unknown

- **Whether a verdict survives the pool's own revision history is not addressed.** The deposit versions the artefact as a whole — the pages travel inside it as one `wiki-pages.zip` file — so a page-level revision is not addressable within the deposit (the record's own `"revision": 2` is Zenodo's deposit revision, char 3667 of the S087 capture); no source opened here says how a claim whose evidence sentence was edited upstream is handled.
- **The 72.36% recall is measured against the super-annotators' evidence set, not against a truth.** The paper says their search covered "every possible sentence" (char 20059), which supports calling the set exhaustive in scope; the word "exhaustive" itself occurs 0 times in the S086 capture (`grep -c exhaustive`), and what a human judge would add beyond that search was not measured.
- **Whether FEVER's authors considered a per-evidence revision id and rejected it is not stated** by any source opened here.

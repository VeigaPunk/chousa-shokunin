#### Inference

- **A Chinese drug regulator's guideline makes whether grey literature was searched a field of the evaluation report, not only a step of the search.** `S124` places `是否检索灰色文献等关键问题` inside the report's 文献检索策略 item, beside the language-restriction and hand-search questions, and separately tells the searcher to collect unpublished material in its 全面查找证据 step; the issuer is attested by `S125` (`国家药品监督管理局发布通告`) and `S126` (`NMPA organized the formulation`), not by the `.doc`. The guideline's verb is 应当 (`应当说明是否有检索限制`), so a lane row should say the report should state the field, not that the guideline enforces it.
- **The reason given is a bias argument, so the field is about completeness of evidence, not about a source list.** `S124` ties grey literature to unpublished negative results (`灰色文献中可能包含阴性研究结果`) and to publication bias when only published literature is included (`会引入发表偏倚`); a search record that omits this field leaves a reader unable to judge that bias, which is the method point this entry adds to the lane's grey-literature-as-source-category entries.
- **The text the lane quotes is a third-party copy whose identity is fixed by two independent issuance records.** The `.doc` carries the title and `（试行）` (`S124`) but no date or issuer stamp of its own; the regulator's newspaper (`S125`, `6月18日，国家药品监督管理局发布通告`) and the regulator's own English page (`S126`, `which was issued on June 18, 2019`) agree on the title, the date and the five-part structure, so the copy's identity is corroborated by document while its text is evidence only of what this copy carries.

#### Inference

- **The Ⅰ级/A beside grey literature grades the recommendation to search that source, not grey literature as evidence.** The draft prints one grade after the whole 7.2.3.3 list (`[31,35,79-82]`, `S127`), and the published table attaches Ⅰ级 and A to each row of its source list (`S129`); on the guideline's own scale `推荐，证据极有效（证据等级为Ⅰ级）` is a recommendation strength, so a curator may cite the guideline as recommending a grey-literature search, never as rating unpublished studies as top-grade evidence.
- **The requirement reaches Chinese drug-evaluation practice through a society guideline, not through the national management guideline.** The workflow guideline is a 中国药师协会 project (`由中国药师协会平台立项`, `S128`) whose published table lists `灰色文献` as a source (`S129`), while the centres' management guideline on nhc.gov.cn holds no grey-literature wording in its captured text form (`药品临床综合评价管理指南`, `S130`); the grading system it adopts is named (`采用中国循证医学中心的证据分级体系进行证据分级评价`, `S129`), so the source list travels with the guideline, not with a regulator's text.
- **A search record built on both national-centre and society instruments meets two pulls on the same unpublished study.** The society guideline recommends `未公开发表的研究信息` as a source (`S129`); the centre's issued quality-control check asks whether an included academic source is published in a peer-reviewed journal (`S118`, its 指标 2 quoted above); a review that includes grey literature under the first should record that choice where the second's 指标 2 is answered.

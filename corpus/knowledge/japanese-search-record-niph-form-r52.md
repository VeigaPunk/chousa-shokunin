- **A search record for this form carries a start and an end date, and the end is bounded by the procedure.** `S115` requires "SRにおける文献検索期間について明記する。" and places the end "分析枠組みが決定された後から製造販売業者による分析提出までの一時点に決める。", so a lane search log for such a submission records a window and can check its end date against two procedural dates, not only against the run date.
- **The form names what was searched and how, through PRISMA.** `S115` lists "使用したデータベース" and "使用した検索式" under the "PRISMA(Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses)声明" lead, so the database list and the expressions are items of the submitted report; it does not set a format for them.

- **The form does not make the searcher a field; the people its body asks to name are outside experts, whose names are in principle not published.** `S115` asks for "その名前と所属、役割、利益相反等を記載すること。" only for experts asked to advise on or perform the analysis (the cover's `【提出者名】` is the submitter) and says "上記の協力を受けた研究者の個人名や所属については原則として公開しない。"; the `#### Unknown` sweep finds no searcher or search-date term, so this form does not answer whether a Japanese record should name who ran the search.

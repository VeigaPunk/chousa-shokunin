#### Observation

- **The protocol-writing paragraph names PRISMA-P, its size and the search's features.** It says PRISMA-P holds 17 items regarded as the minimum elements of a review protocol (`要素と考えられる，17 項目が含まれている`, `S112`, char 10016), and gives among its examples the search's features: "検索の特徴（検索期間" (`S112`, char 10140) and "検索期間，言語制限" (`S112`, char 10146), beside eligibility criteria and whether unpublished material and abstracts are included; the paragraph goes on that these must be specified (`明記することが`, `S112`, char 10200).
- **The search-strategy step names three fields and gives a reason for keeping them.** "検索した文献データベース名" (`S112`, char 11229), the provider "Ovid などのプロバ" (`S112`, char 11243 — a line wrap in the right-hand column of the two-column capture: `プロバ` ends one line and `イダー名` opens the next), and "検索の開始日と終了日を記載することで" (`S112`, char 11294). The reason follows in the same sentence: "最新の結果を反映しているかどうか" (`S112`, char 11348) and "評価がしやすくなる" (`S112`, char 11457).
- **The step states a requirement for the protocol that includes the search period.** A sentence interleaved across the columns reads `ロトコルには完全な検索式と言語制限や検索期間な` (`S112`, char 11730) and `どを，あらかじめ記載する必要がある` (`S112`, char 11805): the protocol must state the complete search expression, language limits and 検索期間 in advance, in a footnoted sentence that cites a reference; the three record fields above are put as what to record (`記載することで`), a weaker form.
- **The same step carries a second rationale and names a professional party to the search.** The step's text includes a reproducibility rationale (`で再現性を担保する`, `S112`, char 12329) and asks the author to record whether a specialist librarian was consulted: `専門の司書に相談したか` (`S112`, char 13543).

#### Inference

- **The window is a named search feature in this copy.** `検索期間，言語制限` (`S112`) appears among PRISMA-P's search features and `検索の開始日と終了日を記載することで` (`S112`) is what the article says to record, so a lane row can carry a window (start and end) rather than a single search date.
- **The database and the provider are fields separate from the window.** `S112` names `検索した文献データベース名` and the provider beside `検索の開始日と終了日を記載することで`; this article's list for the step has three fields, of which a date range is one.
- **The dates are tied to currency and to updating; the step gives reproducibility as a separate reason.** `最新の結果を反映しているかどうか` and `評価がしやすくなる` (`S112`): recording the dates makes it easier to judge whether the results are current and whether the search was updated; the step's other rationale is `で再現性を担保する` (`S112`), so currency is the dates' reason, not the step's only one.
- **This copy is one society journal's article on how to conduct a review, not a national standard.** `S112` lists `検索の特徴（検索期間` among PRISMA-P's and recommends the three fields; the lane can say this article states the fields and cannot say a Japanese national standard does.
- **The article names a librarian, not a searcher field.** The sweep in `#### Unknown` counts no 検索者 and no 検索担当 in the capture, but `専門の司書に相談したか` (`S112`) shows the article does name the search's professional party; what it does not show is a field in the record for who ran the search, so the question of a searcher field is open here, not closed.

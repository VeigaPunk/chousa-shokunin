#### Observation

- **A peer-reviewed Japanese methods journal reports 検索期間 as a PRISMA-P item.** The protocol-writing paragraph says that PRISMA-P holds 17 items regarded as the minimum elements of a review protocol and names the search's features among them: "検索の特徴（検索期間" (`S112`, char 10140) and "検索期間，言語制限" (`S112`, char 10146), beside unpublished material and abstracts.
- **The search-strategy step names three fields and gives the reason for keeping them.** "検索した文献データベース名" (`S112`, char 11229), the provider "Ovid などのプロバ" (`S112`, char 11243 — the two-column capture splits プロバイダー at the gutter, `プロバ` ending the left line and `イダー名` opening the right), and "検索の開始日と終了日を記載することで" (`S112`, char 11294). The reason follows in the same sentence: "最新の結果を反映しているかどうか" (`S112`, char 11348) and "評価がしやすくなる" (`S112`, char 11457).

#### Inference

- **The window is a named field in this copy.** `検索期間，言語制限` (`S112`) is reported as a PRISMA-P protocol element and `検索の開始日と終了日を記載することで` (`S112`) as what the record must state, so a lane row can carry a window (start and end) rather than a single search date.
- **The database and the provider are fields separate from the window.** `S112` names `検索した文献データベース名` and the provider beside `検索の開始日と終了日を記載することで`; a record carrying only a date range is incomplete by this article's list.
- **The stated rationale is currency, and it is the lane's own recheck.** `最新の結果を反映しているかどうか` and `評価がしやすくなる` (`S112`): the record is kept so that a reader can judge whether the results are current and whether the search has been refreshed since.
- **This copy is one society journal's methods article, not a national standard.** `S112` reports `検索の特徴（検索期間` as PRISMA-P's and recommends the three fields; the lane can say this article requires the field and cannot say a Japanese national standard does.
- **The searcher is not a field in this article.** `検索した文献データベース名` and the dates are the record's identifying fields in `S112`, and the sweep in `#### Unknown` counts no 検索者 and no 検索担当 in the capture, so the article does not name who ran the search.

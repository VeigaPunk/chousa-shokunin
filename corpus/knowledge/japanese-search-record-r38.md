#### Inference

- **The record is a pair, and this form keeps the two halves apart.** One half is the sources with a last-search date per database (`それぞれのデータベース毎に最後に検索した日の日付を記載する。`), the other is the expression as run, filed with the searcher's name on an attachment (`検索者、検索ワード、検索式は別紙様式 (V) -5に示したことを記載する。`). As this copy states it, item 7's body asks for the complete strategy and the searcher, the words and the expression are additionally carried on (V)-5, which must hold the words and expression `実際に使用した` or `同等の詳細なもの` — the rule names what was used or an equivalent detail, and does not exclude a reconstruction outright (S108).
- **The article states the same content as one act, and the two documents differ in instrument.** The article asks for `誰が検索を行ったの` and `その日時，使用した検索式を同時に記録しておく` together; the form splits the sources-and-dates half from the searcher-and-expression half and points to a second sheet. So the two documents state overlapping fields with different force (an advisory sentence and a notification form's rule) and with different dates (the article's `その日時` is the search's date, the form's is the last-search date per database); a lane that adopts the theme should cite the pair rather than either document alone (S107).
- **The lane's own sweep check stops short of what these two documents describe.** `verify-sweeps` re-checks the locations a sweep names for emptiness and never executes the recorded command (the round-5 Frontier paragraph of `corpus/README.md`; the limits paragraph of `SKILL.md`), and its per-item line prints id, where, looked, hits, at, observed and verdict — no searcher field and no expression field. A record carrying `誰が検索を行ったの` and `その日時，使用した検索式を同時に記録しておく` is therefore the artefact that would let a sweep be re-run rather than re-guessed; the gate can measure the named set, not the run (S107). Lane inference, narrowed after reading both lane files.
- **The requirement is a reporting rule, not a measurement contract.** The form can be checked against a manuscript, but neither document says how to establish that the expression was actually run: the rule is `実際に使用した` (or `同等の詳細なもの`), which a reader applies by comparison, and the article's wording is advisory (望ましい). The lane's own `corpus/knowledge/chain-resolution-r3.md` records the same split for its chain mode — it states that the mode cannot check that an exemption is truthful and forces only hops that claim resolved to carry anchors that exist. So adopting the Japanese pair improves what a record states without giving the gate a new check (S108).

## What the clause says

The draft workflow standard S127 carries a `2 规范性引用文件` section whose operative sentence is
`规范性引用而构成本文件必不可少的条款` (char 4709): documents named there become indispensable
terms of the standard by citation. The section lists seven national instruments R1-R7 (the
issuing bodies and 文号/versions as printed at chars 4794-5240).

#### Observation

- S127's clause makes each named document binding through citation: `下列文件中的内容通过文中的规范性引用而构成本文件必不可少的条款` (S127 span 1, char 4709).
- R1, the 2019 notice, is held through an association repost whose title line reads `国家卫生健康委关于开展药品使用监测和临床综合评价工作的通知` and whose 文号 line reads `国卫药政函〔2019〕80号` (S131 spans 1-2, chars 271 and 352).
- R2, the 2021 notice, is held through a second association repost: its document title `国家卫生健康委办公厅关于规范开展药品临床综合评价工作的通知_中国中药协会` and the operative sentence `为进一步贯彻落实党中央、国务院关于健全药品供应保障制度的决策部署` (S132 spans 1-2, chars 68 and 1036).
- R3, the 2021 management guideline, is held as the 16号 notice's attachment: the title `药品临床综合评价管理指南` and the edition line `2021 年版试行` (S133 spans 1-2, chars 19 and 54).
- R7, the 2024 quality-control guideline, was already held: the drafting-history sentence `内容，形成《药品临床综合评价质量控制指南（2024 年版 讨论` (S118 span 1, char 3334) and the date line `二〇二四年十二月` (S118 span 2, char 78).

#### Inference

- A user of the draft standard is bound to all seven instruments, not only the ones this lane holds: the clause says the listed content `通过文中的规范性引用而构成本文件必不可少的条款` (S127), and S127 itself names R1 as `国家卫生健康委《关于开展药品使用监测和临床综合评价工作的通知》` (S127, S131).
- The four held instruments chain by document: R1's 文号 `国卫药政函〔2019〕80号` (S131) is the deployment the 2021 notice implements via `为进一步贯彻落实党中央、国务院关于健全药品供应保障制度的决策部署` (S132), which in turn issues the `2021 年版试行` management guideline (S133) that the 2024 quality-control guideline `内容，形成` its own instrument under (S118).
- The three unbound technical guidelines （抗肿瘤， 心血管病 and 儿童 技术指南） are named in S127's `规范性引用文件` clause, but the lane holds no copy of any of them whose defined text form prints its title as a heading line; binding them is refused for that stated reason, not for absence of effort (the sweep lines above).
